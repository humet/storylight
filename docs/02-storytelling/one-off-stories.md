# One-Off Stories

## Purpose

A one-off story is a complete bedtime narrative designed for one reading session.

## Inputs

Required:

- story idea
- selected characters
- saved parent preferences

Optional:

- tone
- theme
- target reading time
- world
- recurring companion
- maximum suspense

## Planning

Even a one-off story must be planned before prose generation.

The plan includes:

- setting
- protagonist desire
- obstacle
- emotional theme
- 6–10 story beats
- climax
- resolution
- calming close
- prohibited outcomes

## Scope

A one-off should not feel like a compressed ten-chapter series.

Prefer:

- one primary setting or a small number of locations
- a limited supporting cast
- one central problem
- one emotional movement
- three to five illustrations

## Length

Suggested defaults:

- Short: 5–7 minutes
- Standard: 8–12 minutes
- Long: 12–15 minutes

Word-count policy is calculated from reading time and reading-age settings.

## Ending

The central desire or problem must resolve. Do not add an unresolved sequel hook merely to encourage another generation.

A recurring world or character may return later, but the story itself remains complete.

## Persistent memory

A one-off may use existing long-term character and world memory.

New generated details do not automatically become permanent family memory. Only conservative reusable facts or parent-approved additions may persist.

## Review

The reviewer checks:

- complete arc
- age appropriateness
- bedtime suitability
- characterisation
- unsupported real-world claims
- repetition
- satisfying resolution
- no sequel dependency

## Acceptance criteria

- The story can be enjoyed without prior context.
- It resolves in one session.
- It has a planned emotional movement.
- It ends calmly.
- It does not corrupt persistent character memory.
