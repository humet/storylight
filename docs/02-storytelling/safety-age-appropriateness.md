# Safety and Age Appropriateness

## Purpose

Storylight must be safe and emotionally suitable for children while preserving imagination and meaningful adventure.

## Safety is layered

Apply safety:

1. At input validation.
2. During planning.
3. During writing.
4. During review.
5. During image generation.
6. During image review.
7. Through parent reporting.

No single filter is sufficient.

## Parent configuration

Store:

- reading age range
- maximum suspense
- excluded topics
- whether mild peril is allowed
- whether death or grief themes are allowed
- whether real family members may appear
- whether school and home details may be fictionalised

## Bedtime suitability

A bedtime chapter may include:

- mystery
- uncertainty
- mild disagreement
- bounded danger
- sadness with support
- age-appropriate fear

It should avoid:

- graphic harm
- prolonged helplessness
- unresolved abandonment
- humiliating moral punishment
- maximum-danger cliffhangers
- frightening visual detail
- adult themes

## Transformation rather than rejection

Many rough child ideas can be safely transformed.

Example:

> “A monster eats everyone.”

Can become:

> A hungry monster keeps trying to eat the village picnic until the children help it ask politely and make enough food for everyone.

The system should preserve imaginative energy without scolding.

## Sensitive real-world details

Do not invent:

- diagnoses
- family conflict
- school incidents
- private addresses
- fears presented as factual
- protected personal characteristics
- traumatic history

## Review severity

Blocking:

- unsafe content
- sexualised child depiction
- graphic injury
- discriminatory content
- adult themes
- explicit self-harm or abuse content
- unsafe instructional behaviour
- severe emotional distress without resolution

Major:

- excessive suspense
- inappropriate vocabulary or concept
- frightening close
- moralising shame

## Parent override

Parents may choose stricter settings. They cannot disable core child-safety rules.

## Acceptance criteria

- Safety failures never publish.
- Parent exclusions flow through every stage.
- The system can safely transform many unsuitable ideas.
- Series chapters end with emotional reassurance.
- Image review treats age drift and sexualisation as blocking.
