# Storylight Company Philosophy

## We build memories, not software

The product exists to help families spend meaningful time together. A child will not remember the model or framework. They may remember the lantern beneath the oak tree, the joke Dad had to read twice, or the night a series finally revealed its secret.

## The parent is the customer; the child is the audience

Parents need confidence, control, and simplicity. Children need warmth, wonder, and familiarity.

## Wonder over spectacle

Small discoveries are often more magical than constant explosions. Storylight should prefer emotionally meaningful details to relentless escalation.

## Calm is a feature

Most software competes for attention. Storylight should gently release it. The product is successful when a family closes the book satisfied.

## Books inspire us more than apps

Books teach pacing, silence, page composition, anticipation, and reread value. Product decisions should learn from publishing before copying generic software patterns.

## Simplicity requires discipline

Tired parents should not have to configure narrative systems. Strong defaults and progressive disclosure are essential.

## Build for the 500th story

Every solution should be judged for long-term trust, maintainability, and familiarity—not only demo impact.

## Definition of success

Success is reflected in:

- stories reread
- series completed
- children asking to continue tomorrow
- low parent regeneration for quality problems
- stable character identity
- families retaining Storylight as a routine
